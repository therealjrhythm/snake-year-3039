# Visual implementation contract

The two supplied 1672 × 941 approved references remain the authority, subject to PRD v2 chapter 4's explicitly raised gameplay camera and corrected HUD/controls. Original files stay untouched in the builder package.

- Scene: true 3D armored articulated serpent, dark metallic rooftop, reflective cyan grid, dimensional energy objects, magenta hostile technology and layered towers. A generated distant skyline complements actual foreground geometry; it is not the arena.
- Color lock: scene #06111D, panels #0B1B2B, cyan #20DFFF, magenta #EA39F5, violet #8957FF, hostile #FF426F, Overdrive #32EDA0, text #E5F5FF, muted #93A9BA.
- Typography: licensed Orbitron for the live title and short numeric/display marks; Rajdhani for readable interface text. Avoid distorted text in functional controls.
- Composition: cinematic live title scene with left-aligned title/primary navigation. Angular translucent bordered buttons; cyan neutral focus, magenta primary action. Settings occupies the right in the wide reference and becomes a focused dialog at constrained viewports.
- Gameplay: whole arena inside a fixed elevated camera; north stays up. Compact top objective/score and bottom integrity/boost/tactical HUD. No permanent large logo, duplicate input hints, or redundant minimap.
- All controls are HTML buttons, inputs, labels and dialogs above the canvas. No text/control assets baked into game imagery. Visible keyboard focus and controller navigation share actions.
- First build supplies only reachable implemented play flows; remaining full launch systems are tracked in the feature matrix without fake mode buttons or pretending they are progression locks.

Review at the source 1672 × 941 viewport and 1280 × 720 minimum; compare head silhouette, armor detail, materials, grid, skyline depth, typography, panel geometry, and legible controls. Owner approval and physical controller evidence cannot be replaced with screenshots or automated tests.
